CURATED KNOWLEDGE SOURCES (prototype) - provenance, licence and review state
==========================================================================

These are authentic public-health publications. They are NOT clinically reviewed for
this app (review_status: draft, clinical_review: none), they are retrievable only under
the TEST-ONLY safety policy, and they must never be presented as clinician-approved.
Publisher authenticity, suitability for a prototype and clinical approval are three
different things; only the first two have been checked, by engineering.

Ingest (as the database owner, never as app_backend):
    python -m app.knowledge.ingest app/knowledge/sources

1. who-doing-what-matters-en.md
   Publisher: World Health Organization
   Title: Doing What Matters in Times of Stress: An Illustrated Guide (2020)
   ISBN 9789240003927 - https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240003927
   Licence: CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/igo/)
   Use: verbatim excerpts of the five skill summaries (Tools 1-5), non-commercial, attributed.
   Changes: capitalised emphasis removed; each excerpt prefixed with its skill name.
   Text source: WHO IRIS text of the 2022 South Sudan adaptation (HealthRight Uganda),
   in which only the illustrations were modified. WHO does not endorse this app.

2. who-doing-what-matters-ar.md
   Publisher: World Health Organization (official Arabic edition)
   Title: القيام بما يهم في أوقات الضغط النفسي: دليل مصور (2020)
   ISBN 978-92-4-001167-0 (electronic)
   Licence: CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO
   Use: excerpts of the five skill summaries, non-commercial, attributed.
   Changes: PDF text-extraction artefacts corrected by hand (broken ligatures such as
   "في", dropped alif in lam-alif); not certified verbatim. WHO does not endorse this app.

3. nimh-caring-for-your-mental-health-en.md
   Publisher: National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH)
   Title: Caring for Your Mental Health (last reviewed April 2026)
   https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/caring-for-your-mental-health
   Licence: public domain (NIMH website text; cite NIMH as the source; images excluded)
   Use: attributed summary with short quotations.

4. nimh-so-stressed-out-en.md
   Publisher: National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH)
   Title: I'm So Stressed Out! Fact Sheet (NIH Publication No. 20-MH-8125)
   https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/publications/so-stressed-out-fact-sheet
   Licence: public domain (cite NIMH as the source)
   Use: the coping list as short quotations. Deliberately excluded: the US-only crisis
