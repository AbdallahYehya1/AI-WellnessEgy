What meditation and mindfulness are (NCCIH). "The term 'meditation' refers to a variety of practices that focus on mind and body integration," used to calm the mind and enhance wellbeing. Mindfulness is a form of meditation that means maintaining "attention or awareness on the present moment without making judgments."

What research says about mindfulness (NCCIH). NCCIH summarises studies suggesting that mindfulness approaches can help with short-term stress, anxiety and low mood, and that mindfulness meditation improved sleep quality more than education-based approaches. It also notes that much of the research is preliminary or not rigorous, so results may be overstated, and that few studies found lasting effects.

Is meditation safe (NCCIH). Meditation and mindfulness are generally considered low risk, but few studies have looked at possible harms; in one review about 8 percent of participants reported a negative effect, most often anxiety or low mood. NCCIH advises not using meditation to replace conventional care or to delay seeing a health care provider, and suggests asking about an instructor's training and experience.
