Benefits (NIDDK). Regular physical activity improves health, mood, and quality of life. Being more active may help you manage your weight.

Keep Moving (NIDDK). Tracking your progress can help you keep moving. Be safe—start slowly, drink plenty of liquids, and talk with your health care professional if you have a health problem or an injury. Choose activities you enjoy and try new ones. Reward yourself.
