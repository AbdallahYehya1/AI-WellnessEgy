Physical activity, mood and sleep (CDC). "Some benefits of physical activity for brain health happen right after a session of moderate-to-vigorous physical activity," including "reduced short-term feelings of anxiety for adults." Regular physical activity "can also reduce your risk of depression and anxiety and help you sleep better."

How much physical activity adults need (CDC). "If you are not physically active, work your way up to 150 minutes a week of moderate-intensity physical activity," for example 30 minutes a day, 5 days a week.
