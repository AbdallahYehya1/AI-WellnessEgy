Signs of work-related stress (NHS). If you're feeling stressed at work, you may notice changes in your emotions, thoughts or behaviour: feeling withdrawn, losing motivation, feeling less confident; jumping to conclusions without having all the facts, making problems feel much bigger than they are, blaming yourself when things go wrong; avoiding certain tasks, working longer hours or weekends, working during holidays.

Work out what you find stressful (NHS). Try to understand what's causing stress as it can help you decide what to do next. Questions to ask yourself: do you have too much to do? do you feel like there isn't enough time? is there a lot of change or uncertainty? are you finding relationships with colleagues difficult? If it helps, write down what was happening when you started to feel stressed.

Focus on what you can change (NHS). Once you know what's making you feel stressed, think about what you can change and what you cannot. For example, you may not be able to change a colleague's personality or behaviour. But you may be able to: change how you respond, set boundaries, ask your manager for support.

See things differently (NHS). The way we see a situation can affect how we feel. These questions can help you change the way you look at situations: is there another way to look at this? can I learn from this situation? could this person be going through something difficult outside of work? This does not mean ignoring a difficult situation. It can help you think more clearly and decide what to do next.

Talk to someone you trust at work (NHS). Talking about how you feel can make a difficult situation feel more manageable. You could speak to: your manager, a colleague you trust, your workplace wellbeing or HR team, a friend or family member. If work is causing stress, your manager may be able to help you agree priorities, adjust your workload or find other ways to support you.

Set boundaries at work (NHS). Boundaries are limits you set to protect your time, energy and wellbeing. You could: take breaks, finish work on time, say no when you already have too much to do, switch off work notifications outside your working hours, agree on realistic deadlines and workloads, keep your workspace separate from where you relax, if possible.
