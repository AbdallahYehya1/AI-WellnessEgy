Self-care habits for everyday wellbeing (NIMH). Get regular exercise: "Just 30 minutes of walking every day can boost your mood", and short bouts of activity count toward your total. Eat healthy, regular meals and stay hydrated: "A balanced diet and plenty of water can improve your energy and focus throughout the day", and cutting back on caffeine and alcohol can help some people. Make sleep a priority: "Stick to a schedule, and make sure you're getting enough sleep", and limit screen light before bed because it can make falling asleep harder.

More self-care ideas (NIMH). Try a relaxing activity: "Explore relaxation or wellness programs or apps, which may incorporate meditation", and schedule time for things you enjoy, such as music, reading, nature or low-stress hobbies. Set goals and priorities: decide what must be done now and what can wait, say no to new tasks when you feel overloaded, and notice what you accomplished each day. Practise gratitude: "Remind yourself daily of things you are grateful for." Focus on positivity by noticing and challenging negative or unhelpful thoughts. Stay connected: "Reach out to friends or family members who can provide emotional support and practical help."

When to seek professional help (NIMH). NIMH advises seeking professional help if you have severe or distressing symptoms that last two weeks or more, such as trouble sleeping, changes in appetite or weight, difficulty getting out of bed because of mood, trouble concentrating, losing interest in things you usually enjoy, being unable to do usual tasks, or feeling irritable, frustrated or restless.
