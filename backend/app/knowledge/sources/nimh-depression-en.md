Low mood and depression (NIMH). NIMH describes depression as more than ordinary sadness: "It can cause severe symptoms that affect how a person feels, thinks, and handles daily activities." Common signs include a persistent sad, anxious or "empty" mood, loss of interest or pleasure in hobbies and activities, fatigue or lack of energy, difficulty concentrating or making decisions, and changes in sleep or appetite. This list is general information and cannot be used to diagnose anyone; a health care provider can help.

Things that can help with low mood (NIMH). NIMH suggests: "Try to get physical activity. Just 30 minutes a day of walking can boost your mood." "Try to maintain a regular bedtime and wake-up time." "Eat regular, healthy meals." "Do what you can as you can." "Connect with people." "Delay making important life decisions until you feel better." NIMH also advises avoiding alcohol, nicotine and drugs that were not prescribed for you.
